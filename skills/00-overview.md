## Review Workflow (Quick Reference)

1. **Pass 1 — Structure**: Hook → Setup → Contributions → Related Lit → Body → Application → Conclusion
2. **Pass 2 — Abstract & Intro**: Throat-clearing test, 30-50% compression, purpose-centric framing
3. **Pass 3 — Terminology**: Preference table, precision over generality, hedging audit
4. **Pass 4 — Additions**: Falsification tests? Software? Replication files? Heuristic summaries?
5. **Pass 5 — Line Polish**: Sentence-level checklist, redundant qualifiers, consistent terminology
